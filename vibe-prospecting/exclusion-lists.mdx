Exclusion lists keep the businesses and prospects you never want to see again, such as current customers, competitors, or people you've already contacted, out of every future search. Your workspace has two permanent lists, one for **businesses** and one for **prospects**:

- **Applied everywhere, automatically.** Anyone on a list is left out of all future chat results and exports, for every member of your workspace. That includes chats through the Vibe Prospecting connector in Claude and other AI assistants. You don't need to mention the lists in your requests.
- **Up to 10,000 entries per list.** The Exclusion Lists page shows how much of each list is in use.
- **Nothing else changes.** Adding a list's entries to an exclusion list doesn't delete or modify the original list.

## Where to find them

In the [Hub](/vibe-prospecting/hub), open **Lists** and click **Exclusion Lists** at the top of the page. Switch between the **Businesses** and **Prospects** tabs to see each list.

## Adding to an exclusion list

### From one of your lists

Add every business or prospect in a list you've already exported or uploaded:

- **From the Lists table:** open the row's **⋯** menu and choose **Add to exclusion list**.
- **From the Exclusion Lists page:** click **Add from list** and pick one of your lists.

<Frame caption="Add a whole list from its row menu on the Lists page.">

Before anything is added, a summary shows how many entries were found, how many are already excluded, how many will be added, and the list's size afterwards. Entries already on the list are skipped, so adding the same list twice is safe.

### From the chat

Ask in plain language:

```
Exclude Acme Corp from future results
```

```
Add the prospects from my "Q4 Outreach" list to my exclusion list
```

## Managing your lists

On the Exclusion Lists page, you can:

- **Search** each list by name or ID.
- **Remove** a single entry, select several and click **Remove selected**, or **Clear list** to remove everything on the current tab.

A newly added entry can briefly show only its ID while its name is looked up.

You can also check and edit the lists from the chat:

```
What's on my exclusion list?
```

```
Is Acme Corp on my exclusion list?
```

```
Stop excluding Acme Corp
```

## Exclusion lists vs. one-off exclusions

You can still exclude results for a single search, for example *"Find new VPs of Sales at fintech companies, but exclude anyone in my Q4 Outreach list."* That applies only to that search. Exclusion lists apply to every search, and the two work together.

<Tip>
  Use exclusion lists for entities you'll never want back, such as current customers and competitors. Use a one-off exclusion when you only want to avoid duplicates within a single campaign.
</Tip>
